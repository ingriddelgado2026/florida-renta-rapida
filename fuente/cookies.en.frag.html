Cookie policy

Last updated: November 2026

What cookies are

Cookies and similar technologies (such as your browser's local storage) are small files or pieces of data that a website saves on your device so it can work or remember your preferences.

What this website uses

This website uses technical, strictly necessary storage to work and, only if you tap "Accept", Google Analytics cookies to learn how many people visit it and how they use it. If you choose "Necessary only", Google Analytics is not loaded. We do not use advertising cookies.

Third-party services

When you tap a WhatsApp button you leave this website and go to WhatsApp (Meta Platforms), which applies its own cookies and privacy policy.

If you accept analytics cookies, Google Analytics (Google LLC) is loaded and receives data about your visit such as country, device type and pages viewed, under its privacy policy. This website is hosted on GitHub Pages and uses fonts stored on the site itself.

How to change your choice

You can change your preferences at any time with the button, or delete cookies in your browser settings. If you switch to "Necessary only", we delete this website's Google Analytics cookies.
