1. Who is responsible for your data

Clubs Enterprise, LLC, doing business under the registered trade name Florida Renta Rápida, located at 2175 NW 34th Ave, Miami, FL 33142, United States. For any privacy matter, you can email us at servicios.administrativos@agencysafetravel.com or call {{VENTAS}}.

2. What data we collect

  • Booking form: name, vehicle category and model of interest, pick-up date, number of days, country of residence and whether you have a driver's license.
  • Direct contact: the data you give us when you message us on WhatsApp, email or call us (for example, your number and your messages).
  • To complete the rental: the documents required at vehicle pickup under our terms and conditions: booking confirmation, passport or ID, driver's license, credit or debit card. These documents are not requested or stored on this website.
  • During the rental: the vehicle's location through GPS tracking, toll usage (SunPass or E-ZPass) and any fines or incidents involving the vehicle.
  • Technical visit data: like any website, the service that hosts it (GitHub Pages) receives visitors' IP address and basic browser information for security and operation.

This website does not store form data on any server: when you tap "Send on WhatsApp", the WhatsApp app opens with your message, and you decide whether to send it.

3. How we use it

  • To answer your request and manage your booking and rental agreement.
  • To verify that you meet the rental requirements (age, license, payment method and insurance).
  • To apply the security deposit and charge rental fees, tolls, fines or damages, as set out in the terms and conditions.
  • To assist you during the rental and protect the vehicle (GPS tracking, insurance and 24/7 assistance).
  • To tell you about promotions only if you ask for them or agree to receive them.
  • To comply with legal, accounting and insurance obligations.

4. Why we may use it

We use your data because we need it to provide the service you request (the booking and rental agreement), because you give us your consent when required (for example, for promotions), and to comply with the laws that apply in the state of Florida and the United States.

5. Who we share it with

  • WhatsApp (Meta Platforms): the messaging service we use to assist you. It has its own privacy policy.
  • Insurers, GPS tracking providers and payment processors, only when needed for the service.
  • Toll systems (SunPass or E-ZPass) and traffic authorities, to charge tolls and handle fines incurred during the rental.
  • GitHub, the service that hosts this website.
  • Google Analytics (Google LLC), only if you accept analytics cookies: visit statistics (country, device, pages viewed and bookings sent), without your name or contact details. More in the cookie policy.
  • Authorities, when required by law (for example, accidents, theft or court orders).

We do not sell your data.

6. How long we keep it

Requests that don't become a booking are deleted within 12 months at most. Rental agreement data is kept for as long as tax, accounting and insurance laws require.

7. Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your data, object to its use or withdraw your consent by emailing servicios.administrativos@agencysafetravel.com. We will reply within 30 days at most. We may not be able to delete some data while the law requires us to keep it.

8. Security

This website uses an encrypted connection (HTTPS) and does not store personal data. Rental documents are handled only by authorized staff.

9. Minors

Our services are for people aged 21 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from minors.
