7. Rental policies

Residents

Rentals are not made to residents of the United States, nor to residents of the state of Florida.

Reservations

Customers must be over 21 years old and under 75 years old to rent a vehicle. Customers aged 21 to 24 are charged an additional ${{JOVEN}} per day. If the client is delayed, they must give notice of their late arrival to avoid a cancellation. Debit cards are not accepted. Some rates do not include the following: processing and facilities, license recovery, RDF, surcharge and sales tax (these amounts range from $12.99 daily and up, depending on vehicle model and season).

Insurance

Insurance is mandatory for all our vehicle categories. The following types of insurance are not included in the reservation: Supplemental Liability Insurance (SLI), Collision Damage Waiver (CDW) and Third Party Liability (TPL).

Basic protection: if your car is stolen, you could lose your entire deposit, but as long as you have basic protection, you don't need to pay the deductible if your car is stolen.

MANDATORY Collision Damage Waiver: covers damage to the body of your rental vehicle, as well as any losses the rental company experiences due to the need to repair your vehicle. (DEDUCTIBLE APPLIES).

Customer responsibilities

Customers are responsible for: parking and traffic fines, car keys, flat tires (provided the event occurs during the customer's rental period) and upholstery.

Mileage and territory

Miles are unlimited within the state of Florida. Under no circumstances may the vehicle leave the state of Florida, as this violates our policies and the client will be 100% responsible for tolls and all damages that may be caused to the vehicle.

Cancellation policy

Reservations are held for one hour from the pickup time. Customers who are delayed must contact Florida Renta Rápida to change the pickup time and avoid a cancellation for not showing up on time. If the customer does not show up, no refund will be made. Payments made are non-refundable. If the customer needs to reschedule or has any issue with the reservation, they have up to 12 months to use it.

Florida tolls (SunPass)

The toll system is mandatory in the state of Florida, and there are two ways to get it: by activation and usage, for $14.99 per day plus usage, or unlimited, for $19.99 per day.

Valid driver's licenses issued in your state or country, as well as international licenses, are accepted.
